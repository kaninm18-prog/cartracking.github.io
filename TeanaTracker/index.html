🚗 Teana Tracker

Connect to Google Sheets in 3 steps — no account needed beyond your Gmail.

Step 1 — Create a Google Sheet
1
Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet.
2
Name it Teana Tracker.
3
Copy the Sheet ID from the URL:
docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/SHEET_ID/edit
Step 2 — Set up the Apps Script
1
In your Google Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script.
2
Delete all existing code. Paste in the contents of Code.gs (in the TeanaTracker folder).
3
Replace PASTE_YOUR_SHEET_ID_HERE with your Sheet ID, and PASTE_ANY_PASSWORD_HERE with any password you choose (e.g. teana2025).
4
Click Save (💾), then click Run → setup. Accept permissions when asked. This creates all 4 sheets automatically.
5
Click Deploy → New deployment.
Type: Web app
Execute as: Me
Who has access: Anyone
Click Deploy, then copy the Web app URL.
