Enter Your Azure App Client ID

Follow the steps below to get a free Client ID, then paste it here.

How to get your Client ID
Step 1
Open portal.azure.com on your computer and sign in with your Microsoft account.
Step 2
Search "App registrations" in the top bar → click "+ New registration"
Step 3
Name: Teana Tracker
Account type: Personal Microsoft accounts only
Redirect URI type: Single-page application (SPA)
URI value: Loading...
Step 4
Click Register, then copy the Application (client) ID shown at the top.
Step 5
Go to API permissions → Add a permission → Microsoft Graph → Delegated permissions
Add: Files.ReadWrite and User.Read → click Add permissions
Step 6
Paste the Client ID above and tap Save & Sign In.
